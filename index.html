Who we are

Our Pugulous Story 🐾

Pugs in Mugs started with a simple dream: a place where great coffee meets great people, every single morning. Nestled in Somerville, we've built more than a cafe — we've built a community.

We're a proud family-owned business, and every decision — from the beans in your cup to the smile at the counter — comes straight from the heart. We partner with Frederick's Coffee, Melbourne's own specialty roaster, because we believe in local and we believe in the best.

Here at Pugs in Mugs, everyone is welcome — big, small, two-legged or four. Bring the kids and let them loose in our play area while you enjoy your coffee. We'll spoil the babies with a free babyccino, and every furry mate who visits gets a free pugccino on us. 🐾

Pull up a chair, stay a while, and kick off your day the Pugs way — bring whoever makes your morning better.
